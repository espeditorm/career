**Projeto liderado de ponta a ponta com pouca supervisão:**
```
At Intmed I led the design of the Emergency Triage System for Hapvida, the largest health plan in North and Northeast Brazil. I brought engineering in early, wrote the specs and acceptance criteria myself, and used the research findings to defend priorities with Product when scope got tight.
```
