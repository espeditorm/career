## 4. Respostas abertas (EN)

### Tell us about yourself / Short bio (≈ 90 palavras)
I'm a Senior Product Designer from Brazil with 8+ years designing products in regulated, high-stakes
spaces. Right now I design and specify digital banking features for US credit unions and a Brazilian
federal bank at Integritas. Before that, I led a UX tribe at Intmed, where I designed an emergency
triage system that runs in 150+ hospitals, and at Wipro I redesigned Brazil's largest microcredit
program. I like owning problems end to end, from discovery to the spec an engineer can build from,
and I also teach UX/UI, which keeps me sharp on the fundamentals.

### Why are you interested in this role? (modelo, cada vaga tem a versão pronta)
What caught my attention is [PRODUTO/PROBLEMA DA EMPRESA]. It's the kind of problem I've been solving
for years: complex rules, people under pressure, and a product that has to feel simple anyway. I'd
bring hands-on experience in [ÁREA RELEVANTE] and a habit of working very close to engineering.

### Why are you looking to leave your current job?
I've enjoyed the work at Integritas, but it's a services environment where I move between clients.
I'm looking for a product company where I can own a problem long term, measure the impact of what I
ship and grow with the same team.

### Describe a project you're proud of
At Intmed I designed the Emergency Triage System for Hapvida, the largest health plan in North and
Northeast Brazil. The tools healthcare professionals used were slow and fragmented, and every extra
step mattered in an emergency room. I ran workshops and research with doctors, managers and the
product team, redesigned and restructured the flows, and wrote the specs so engineering could move
without guesswork, which also shortened our sprints. The system is now used in 150+ hospitals, and
the components we built for it became the base of the design system for six medical products.

### How do you work with engineers / PMs?
I bring engineers in early, usually in discovery, so constraints shape the solution instead of
breaking it later. Because I also work as a Business Analyst, my handoffs come with user stories,
acceptance criteria and edge cases, not just screens. After handoff I stay close: I review builds,
join UAT and adjust when reality differs from the file.

### Design systems experience
I've built or evolved design systems at Wipro, Intmed (six medical products, about 30% less design
time) and now at Integritas, where I work across multiple banking systems for web and mobile. I care
about tokens, clear documentation (Zeroheight) and components that engineers actually want to use.

### How do you use AI in your design work?
I use AI to speed up the parts that don't need my judgment: synthesizing research notes, drafting
edge-case lists and acceptance criteria, generating content variations and quick prototypes to test
ideas. My current tools are Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini for thinking and writing, NotebookLM for
research, Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and Google AI Studio for coded prototypes, and Google Stitch and
Figma Make for fast UI exploration. I also teach in an AI extension course at the Federal University
of Ceará, so I spend time thinking about where AI helps users and where it just adds noise.

### How do you measure success of a design?
I agree on the metric before designing: task success, time on task, conversion, support tickets,
whatever fits the problem. I pair that with qualitative signals from usability tests. On Re.tifica,
for example, we tracked SUS and reached an average of 92.5.

### Experience with research
Moderated usability tests, interviews, field studies, surveys, heuristic evaluations and
even eye-tracking studies during university research, which led to published papers.

### Anything else you'd like to share? (campo opcional, use sempre)
My portfolio at studiodito.co has case studies from banking, healthcare and public services. I'm
happy to walk you through any of them. I work comfortably in English with US teams every day.
